## 10,000 votes submitted at once (ScalEVote)

Ours: 9,914/10,000 accepted (412 at the first try), all done in 153.8 s (64.5 votes/s); end-to-end latency 87547.1 +/- 40925.8 ms (max 153341.4 ms).

Published: up to 10,000 concurrent votes with 10 regional servers; average end-to-end latency under 2,300 ms (+/- 1,612 ms).

## Tally of 64 and 1,024 voters (STARK vector-commitment study)

| Voters | Ours: core-minutes | Ours: peak process memory | Published: core-minutes | Published: peak RAM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 64 | 0.00397 | 147.9 MB | 1.15 | 1.38 GB |
| 1,024 | 0.00436 | 149.6 MB | 20.8 | 11.82 GB (Harrison & Haines: 175.41 GB) |

Not like for like: the published figures include generating a STARK proof that the tally is correct; ours are the homomorphic tally plus 3-of-5 threshold decryption, whose correctness anyone can re-check by recomputing the product of the published ciphertexts.

Abuidris et al. (PSC-Bchain): >60 TPS and ~27 s latency at 1,000 simulated nodes — node-count simulation, no matching run on one machine; compare against the throughput and latency rows above.
