Voter Device Benchmark

Runs the phone app's own ballot code on this device: Paillier 2048-bit one-hot encryption, the ballot-validity proof, the ECDSA P-256 device signature and the 14-hash receipt check. Works on iPhone (Safari) and Android (Chrome). Nothing is sent anywhere: the page runs entirely on this phone.

Ready. A full run takes about one to three minutes on a phone.

  • Plug the phone in and turn off Low Power Mode (iPhone) or Battery Saver (Android): both slow the processor down.
  • Close other apps. Stay on this page until it says Finished; the screen is kept on for you.
  • If the page goes to the background, the ballot being timed is discarded and repeated, so the figures stay clean.
